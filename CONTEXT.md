# Domain Glossary

## Knowledge domain

A stable top-level technical object that can own principles across languages and projects. The canonical domains are AI systems, backend and distributed systems, data systems, computer systems and performance, and software construction and quality.

## Topic

A coherent subdivision inside one knowledge domain. A topic groups knowledge points that answer closely related questions; it is not a course, job title, skill level, project, or technology vendor.

## Knowledge point

One canonical explanation of one engineering problem. It states the problem and background, the solution and mechanism, the effects and trade-offs, and the validation boundary. If another page answers the same question, their useful evidence is merged into one canonical knowledge point.

## Skill

A demonstrated level of mastery over knowledge points, such as explaining, implementing, diagnosing, comparing, or validating them. A skill is not a parallel content category and does not own duplicate articles.

## Clipping

Unprocessed source material retained for traceability. A clipping can support a knowledge point but is not itself part of the canonical knowledge structure.

## Project or tool

An implementation, learning environment, or evidence source. Projects and tools link to knowledge points; they do not define the knowledge taxonomy.
